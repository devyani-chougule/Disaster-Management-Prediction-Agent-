FLOOD DISASTER GUIDELINES

Flood causes:
Floods occur due to heavy rainfall, river overflow, poor drainage,
high upstream water accumulation and low elevation regions.

Low flood risk actions:
Continue monitoring rainfall.
Maintain normal preparedness.

Medium flood risk actions:
Monitor weather warnings.
Check emergency supplies.
Avoid unnecessary travel near water bodies.

High flood risk actions:
Immediately monitor evacuation alerts.
Move people from low lying areas.
Prepare emergency shelters.
Keep food, water and medical supplies ready.
Avoid flooded roads.



CYCLONE DISASTER GUIDELINES

Cyclone causes:
Cyclones are influenced by high wind speed,
low atmospheric pressure, pressure drop and ocean temperature.

Low cyclone risk actions:
Monitor weather updates.

Medium cyclone risk actions:
Prepare emergency kits.
Secure household objects.
Follow weather alerts.

High cyclone risk actions:
Evacuate coastal areas.
Move to cyclone shelters.
Disconnect electricity if flooding occurs.
Follow official emergency instructions.



EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Authorities should:
Activate warning systems.
Coordinate evacuation.
Prepare rescue teams.
Open emergency shelters.

Citizens should:
Keep emergency documents safe.
Carry medicines.
Follow government instructions.
Avoid spreading misinformation.
